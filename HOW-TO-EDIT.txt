YOUR BOOK WEBSITE: HOW TO EDIT IT
=================================

Everything you'll ever need to change is in ONE file: settings.js

CHANGE A PRICE
  1. On GitHub, open your website repository and click settings.js
  2. Click the pencil icon (Edit)
  3. Change the number, e.g.  priceZAR: 299,  ->  priceZAR: 279,
  4. Click "Commit changes". The live site updates in about 1 minute.

CHANGE THE COVER
  Easiest way (same file name):
  1. Save your new cover as  cover.jpg
  2. On GitHub, open the "images" folder, then Add file > Upload files
  3. Drag in cover.jpg, then click "Commit changes". It replaces the old one.
  Different file name (e.g. cover-v2.png):
  Upload it to "images", then in settings.js change
     coverImage: "images/cover.jpg",  ->  coverImage: "images/cover-v2.png",
  Tip: a refresh may show the old cover for a minute (browser cache).

CURRENCIES
  Buyers pick ZAR, USD, GBP or EUR at the top of the shop.
  ZAR is paid via PayFast; USD/GBP/EUR via PayPal.
  Each format has a price per currency:
     prices: { ZAR: 299, USD: 18, GBP: 14, EUR: 16 },
  To stop offering a currency, remove it from the  currencies:  line.

LOCAL DELIVERY (South Africa)
  Edit the  deliverySouthAfrica  list: each line is one option the buyer
  can pick, with its price in Rand. Add, remove or re-price any line.

SELLING ON AMAZON (KDP) AS WELL
  When your paperback is live on Amazon, paste its link into
     amazonLink: "",
  for the Paperback. Overseas buyers are then sent to Amazon for it,
  while South Africans still buy direct from you.
  Leave the Signed Paperback's amazonLink empty.
  WARNING: if you put the Kindle eBook in KDP Select / Kindle Unlimited,
  Amazon does not allow you to sell the eBook anywhere else, including
  this site. Then set the eBook to  available: false  here.

EBOOK ORDERS
  When an eBook payment comes in, email the buyer the EPUB file as an
  attachment (the site promises within 24 hours).

TURN ON PAYMENTS
  South Africa (PayFast):
    Paste your Merchant ID and Merchant Key into settings.js.
    Leave payfastTestMode: true and do a test on sandbox first.
    When happy, change it to  payfastTestMode: false,
    IMPORTANT: leave the PayFast "passphrase" blank in your PayFast settings,
    or payments will be refused.
  USD / GBP / EUR (PayPal):
    Put your PayPal business email in  paypalEmail: "",
  Until payments are on, the button says "Reserve my copy by email" and
  sends you an email with the buyer's order and address.

WHERE TO FIND ORDERS
  PayFast: each payment notification shows the buyer's name, email and
  the custom fields: delivery method + address or pickup point (custom_str1), phone (custom_str2),
  signing name (custom_str3) and what they bought (custom_str4).
  ALWAYS check the amount paid in PayFast/PayPal matches the price
  before you post a book.
  PayPal: the delivery address is in the transaction details.

GOLDEN RULES FOR EDITING settings.js
  - Only change text inside "quotes" or the numbers.
  - Keep the comma at the end of each line.
  - Prices are plain numbers: 299 (not R299).
  - If the site goes blank after an edit, a comma or quote is missing.
    Open settings.js, click "History", and compare with the last version.

OTHER FILES (no need to touch)
  index.html  - the page itself (Chapter One text lives here)
  style.css   - colours and layout
  app.js      - the shop logic
  thanks.html - the page buyers see after paying
